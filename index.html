Features

Go from a block of text to a listening queue you can take anywhere.

  • Text to Speech

    Turn the text you provide into spoken audio.

  • Playback Queue

    Line up several texts and rearrange what plays next.

  • Follow Along

    See the current passage highlighted as it’s read aloud.

  • Tap-to-Seek and Text Seek

    Tap within the text or use Text Seek to jump to another point.

  • Image OCR

    Extract text from images on your device and review it before listening.

  • Listen Later

    Save text on your device and play it when you’re ready.

  • Background playback

    Keep listening while using other apps or with the screen locked.

  • Voice and speed controls

    Choose an available voice and adjust the reading speed.

How it works

  1. Share or paste text

    Share selected text from another app, paste it into LoudThread, or extract it from an image with on-device OCR.

  2. Decide when to listen

    Play it now, add it to the playback queue, or save it for later.

  3. Listen hands-free

    Start playback and carry on, even when you switch apps or lock your screen.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is LoudThread?

An Android app that reads aloud articles, notes, stories, and other text you share or paste.

Does LoudThread work offline?

Offline narration depends on the offline-capable voices installed on your device. Ads and purchases require an internet connection where applicable.

Do I need an account?

You can use the app without a LoudThread account.

Can I listen with my screen locked?

Yes. Background playback continues while the screen is locked.

Can I extract text from images?

Yes. On-device OCR lets you extract text from an image and review it before listening.

How does Premium Lifetime work?

A one-time Google Play purchase permanently removes ads. There’s no subscription. Google Play shows the price in your region.

Is LoudThread affiliated with Reddit?

LoudThread is an independent app with no affiliation with or endorsement from Reddit.
